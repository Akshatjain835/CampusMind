GRADING, EVALUATION & ACADEMIC STANDING REGULATIONS
===================================================================================
CampusMind Academic Governance Policy | Effective Academic Year 2025-2026

1. GRADING SCALE & GRADE POINT AVERAGE (GPA/CGPA)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Performance is evaluated on a 10-point letter grading scale:
  • O (Outstanding - 90% to 100%): 10 Grade Points
  • A+ (Excellent - 80% to 89%): 9 Grade Points
  • A (Very Good - 70% to 79%): 8 Grade Points
  • B+ (Good - 60% to 69%): 7 Grade Points
  • B (Above Average - 55% to 59%): 6 Grade Points
  • C (Average - 50% to 54%): 5 Grade Points
  • P (Pass - 40% to 49%): 4 Grade Points
  • F (Fail - Below 40%): 0 Grade Points (Backlog)

- SGPA & CGPA Calculation:
  • SGPA = Sum of (Course Credits × Grade Points) / Total Semester Credits.
  • Minimum CGPA required for degree award is 5.0 with zero active backlogs.

2. RE-EVALUATION & ANSWER SCRIPT TRANSPARENCY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Answer Script Inspection: Students may apply for physical/digital copy of evaluated mid-sem/end-sem answer sheets within 7 days of result declaration.
- Re-evaluation Fee: ₹500 per paper. If marks increase by more than 10%, fee is refunded.
- Re-evaluation Verdict: Higher of original or re-evaluated marks will be awarded.

3. REMEDIAL EXAMINATIONS & BACKLOG CLEARANCE
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Remedial (Supplementary) Exams are conducted within 30 days of semester result declaration.
- Eligibility for Remedial: Students awarded 'F' grade or students detaining due to medical condonation.
- Maximum grade achievable in a Remedial Examination is 'A' (Grade Point 8).

SCHOLARSHIPS, RESEARCH GRANTS & STUDENT FINANCIAL AID
===================================================================================
CampusMind Merit & Need-Based Student Support Regulations

1. MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS & TUITION WAIVERS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Director's Gold Medal Scholarship: 100% tuition waiver for 1st rank holder in each department.
- Merit-Cum-Means Scholarship: 50% tuition waiver for CGPA >= 8.5 with annual family income < ₹6.0 Lakhs.
- Attendance Eligibility Criteria: Minimum 85% attendance required in all subjects to maintain scholarship.

2. HACKATHON & CONFERENCE TRAVEL GRANTS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- National Hackathons (Smart India Hackathon, Techfest): Reimbursement of 2nd AC train fare + ₹3,000/team allowance.
- International IEEE/ACM Conference Paper Presentation: Grant up to ₹50,000 for registration & travel.
- Application Workflow: Apply via AI Secretary -> Recommendation by Faculty Advisor -> HOD Sanction.

3. INNOVATION & PROTOTYPE DEVELOPMENT FUND
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Student Startup Seed Grant: Funding up to ₹1,00,000 for approved hardware/software prototypes.
- Managed by Institute Innovation Council (IIC) & AI Incubation Cell.

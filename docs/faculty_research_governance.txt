==============================================================================
DEPARTMENT OF COMPUTER SCIENCE & ENGINEERING - FACULTY & RESEARCH GOVERNANCE
==============================================================================

SECTION 1: AICTE & NBA TEACHING WORKLOAD NORMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 1.1: Weekly Workload Distribution
- Professor / HOD: 12-14 teaching hours per week + 8 administrative governance hours.
- Associate Professor: 14-16 teaching hours per week + 4 departmental committee hours.
- Assistant Professor: 16-18 teaching hours per week + lab coordination duties.

Clause 1.2: Course File & Outcome Documentation
- Course files (Syllabus, Lesson Plan, CO-PO Matrix, Question Banks, Sample Answer Sheets) must be finalized 7 days prior to semester commencement.
- Monthly attendance verification must be submitted to the HOD office by the 5th of every month.

SECTION 2: RESEARCH & PUBLICATION MANDATES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 2.1: Annual Publication Criteria (NBA / NAAC Requirement)
- Every faculty member must publish at least 1 research paper per academic year in an SCI, Scopus, or IEEE indexed journal/conference.
- Publication incentives of up to Rs. 15,000 per SCI paper are provided by the department research council.

Clause 2.2: Conference Sponsorship & FDP Participation
- Faculty members are eligible for financial support (registration fee + DA) to present papers at up to 2 Scopus-indexed national/international conferences per year.
- Minimum 1 Faculty Development Program (FDP) of 5 days duration per year is mandatory.

SECTION 3: ACCREDITATION & AUDIT RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clause 3.1: NAAC & NBA Internal Audit
- Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) conducts biannual departmental reviews.
- Continuous assessment registers, lab logbooks, and student feedback reports (minimum 80% positive score target) must be ready for inspection.
